amaraMusicVisualiser

This local macOS build runs the visualiser on your device.
Start the app and choose a local audio file, a microphone, or a supported system-audio source.
macOS may ask for microphone or system-audio recording permission.

For source setup, optional font details and licensing, see the repository README.md.
For public binary distribution, sign and notarize with your own Developer ID and verify on a clean Mac.
